Hi! I am a recent Computational Linguistics Masters graduate from Michtom School of Computer Science at Brandeis University. Previously, I received a Linguistics and Mathematical and Scientific Computation double major from the University of California, Davis.

I am seeking opportunities in improving language model interpretability and reliability, focusing mainly on the application of Computational Semantics in deep learning architecture.

## What I'm up to
I will be attending ACL 2026 and presenting a poster at SemEval. Please come say hello!

## What I am Outside of Work
I am a creative at heart. I draw and paint -- both digitally and traditionally -- everything from cartoons to landscapes.

I love the wilderness! I am a chronic climber, hiker, camper, and backpacker. In July 2025, I completed the John Muir Trail, completing 210 miles of High Sierra terrain.
