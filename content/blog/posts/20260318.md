# Tech Stack 
After graduating from my Computational Linguistics Masters, I discovered that my program left my unsure how to make my own apps. I did relatively well in my program and excelled at the tools left to me by my professors. Here I want give a detailed overview of the missing tech stack for these more theoritical majors.

## My journey here

Majoring in pure Linguistics in college, I was missing the ability to do a lot of quantitative analysis of my work. Yeah I understood the basic software, like Praat, AntConc, and transcription standards, like CHILDES. My hope coming out of Masters was to gain the confidence to write programs dealing with words and sentences. However, this is when ChatGPT took over the internet and in effect the field of Computational Linguistics. On the surface, decades old problems such as part of speech (POS) tagging, word tokenization, and machine translation (MT) were all seemingly solved by the implementation of attention.
