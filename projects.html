Headline Political Spectrum Classifier (2023)

  • Developed a Naive Bayes model from scratch to classify the political spectrum of news article headlines, using scraped data from five major news outlets using BeautifulSoup and the sitemap.xml of each outlet.
  • Implemented a term frequency-inverse document frequency matrix as a feature input to minimize the influence of low-impact tokens that were tokenized using spaces.
